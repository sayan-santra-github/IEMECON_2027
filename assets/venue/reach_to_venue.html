Reach to Venue

Your Journey to the Venue Starts Here

Experience a smooth journey to the Rajasthan International Centre, Jaipur.

From Jaipur Junction

Distance: Approximately 10 km
Travel Time: 25 - 35 minutes
Best Option: App-based cabs (Ola/Uber) or prepaid taxis are readily available outside the station.
Directions: Head south toward Jhalana Doongri.
Get Detailed Directions

From Jaipur Airport (JAI)

Distance: Approximately 6.6 km
Travel Time: 25 minutes
Best Option: Pre-paid taxi counter inside the terminal or app-based services (Uber/Ola).
Directions: Follow Airport Rd to Jawahar Lal Nehru Marg, then Jhalana Doongri.
Get Detailed Directions
